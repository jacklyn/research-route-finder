    When to ask for help

    Asking UXR for help doesn’t mean handing over the study.

    Grab Research when:

    • you can’t clearly articulate the research question
    • the findings will support a significant product or investment decision
    • you’re entering an unfamiliar problem space
    • recruiting is complicated
    • you need to make quantitative claims
    • results are contradictory and you’re not sure how to interpret them
    • the topic is particularly sensitive
    • you need deeper probing than an unmoderated study can provide

    Sometimes a 15–20 minute conversation before launch is all you need.
